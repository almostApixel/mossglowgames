Tidelight

A cozy diving game

A storm took the harbour. Pilot a little sub down a new reef every dive, net fish, fill a walk-in aquarium and rebuild the harbour one building at a time, until the lighthouse shines again.

  • 91 species to catch and show off in your own aquarium
  • Seven zones and a fresh reef every dive
  • Plays fully on a controller and on Steam Deck
